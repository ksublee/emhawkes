### Examples

Examples organized by kernel structure, marks and state dependence.

- [Extended
  examples](https://ksublee.github.io/emhawkes/articles/extended_example.md):

  Choose examples by kernel structure, marks, state-dependent baselines
  or state-dependent impact.

- [Multi-kernel models and shared
  parameters](https://ksublee.github.io/emhawkes/articles/multi-kernel-models.md):

  Multiple exponential kernels, event-to-column mappings and shared
  model parameters.

- [Marked
  models](https://ksublee.github.io/emhawkes/articles/marked-models.md):

  Mark-dependent excitation, linear impact and estimation of mark
  distributions.

- [State-dependent baseline
  models](https://ksublee.github.io/emhawkes/articles/state-dependent-baseline.md):

  A spread model and a Hawkes/M/infinity queue with count-dependent
  baseline intensities.

- [State-dependent impact
  models](https://ksublee.github.io/emhawkes/articles/state-dependent-impact.md):

  Hawkes flocking and bid-ask price models with state-dependent
  excitation and component resets.

### Guides

Estimation, interpretation and calculation options.

- [Fitting and
  inference](https://ksublee.github.io/emhawkes/articles/fitting-and-inference.md):

  Parameter settings, initial states, fitted models, residual
  diagnostics and standard errors.

- [Computation](https://ksublee.github.io/emhawkes/articles/computation.md):

  Likelihood engines, analytic gradients, Jacobians and performance.

- [Shared exponential kernel
  calculations](https://ksublee.github.io/emhawkes/articles/shared-kernel.md):

  Replay numeric exponential states through a public,
